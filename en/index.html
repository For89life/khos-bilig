About us

Nine years built together

We protect your security to a professional standard.

Khos Bilig Security Holding LLC is a professional organization that strictly observes the Constitution of Mongolia, the Law on Contract Guarding and Security and all related laws and regulations, and that is now in its ninth year of steady, uninterrupted operation in the security industry.

We see security not merely as a guarding duty but as a strategic component protecting our client's assets, people, business continuity and reputation. We therefore plan each service around the client's circumstances, the site's risk level and the needs of their operation, and deliver it to a high professional standard.

We work to the national standard MNS 6908:2021 “Security and guarding services”, delivering our service through modern methods, qualified personnel and accountable management.

Over the past five years we have guarded high-risk sites with special requirements, steadily building our industry experience and professional capability — mining operations, processing plants, coal camps and business centers among them.

Our goal is not simply to guard a site, but to be a dependable partner in our client's safe and stable operation.

Liability system & insurance

Protection backed by guaranteed liability coverage

To reduce the risk of damage to third parties we maintain a General Liability Insurance Agreement with Munkh Insurance LLC. It covers risks to the life, health and property of third parties: where an investigation determines that the damage was caused by our security service, up to 90% of the resulting loss will be compensated.
